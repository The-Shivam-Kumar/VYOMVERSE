ABOUT VYOMVERSE

A free site that explains the universe in easy words.

OUR AIM

Make space easy to understand

What this site is

A learning site. It tells the story of the universe from the Big Bang to today.

How we write

In short, simple sentences. Hard ideas are explained with everyday words.

Accuracy

Numbers are rounded and based on well-known science. Guesses and ideas are marked as such.

Keeping up

Space news changes fast. Check NASA, ESA and ISRO for new facts.

Pictures

All pictures are made with code, so they load fast and always work.

The name

VYOMVERSE joins "vyom", the Sanskrit word for sky, and "universe".
