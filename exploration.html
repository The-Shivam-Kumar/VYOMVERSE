CHAPTER 4

EXPLORATION

From people who watched the sky with their eyes to robots on Mars and a telescope that sees the first galaxies.

Before rocketsSpace AgeShuttles and stationsRobot explorersToday

BEFORE 1957

Sky watchers and telescopes

  1. About 270 BCE

    Aristarchus

    A Greek thinker suggests that Earth goes around the Sun. People ignore the idea for nearly 2,000 years.

  2. 476 CE

    Aryabhata

    The Indian astronomer is born. He later explains that Earth spins on its axis and that eclipses come from shadows.

  3. 1543

    Copernicus

    Publishes a Sun-centred model of the solar system.

  4. 1609

    Kepler and Galileo

    Kepler shows that planets move in ovals. Galileo points a telescope at the sky and in 1610 sees Jupiter's moons.

  5. 1687

    Newton

    Explains gravity and why planets move the way they do.

  6. 1781 and 1846

    New planets

    Herschel finds Uranus. Neptune is found by maths first.

  7. 1915

    Einstein

    General relativity: gravity is curved space and time.

  8. 1929

    Hubble

    Shows that the universe is growing.

1957 TO 1975

The Space Age begins

  1. 1957

    Sputnik 1

    The Soviet Union launches the first satellite on 4 October.

  2. 1961

    Yuri Gagarin

    The first person in space goes around Earth on 12 April.

  3. 1965

    First spacewalk

    Alexei Leonov floats outside his spacecraft.

  4. 1969

    Apollo 11

    Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin land on the Moon on 20 July.

  5. 1971

    First space station

    Salyut 1 starts orbiting Earth.

  6. 1972

    Apollo 17

    The last time people walked on the Moon, so far.

  7. 1975

    Aryabhata

    India launches its first satellite.

1976 TO 1999

Probes, shuttles and stations

  1. 1976

    Viking

    Two landers look for signs of life on Mars.

  2. 1977

    Voyager 1 and 2

    Two probes start a tour of the outer planets. Voyager 1 entered space between the stars in 2012.

  3. 1981

    Space Shuttle

    The first reusable crewed spaceship flies.

  4. 1984

    Rakesh Sharma

    The first Indian goes to space, on a Soviet Soyuz.

  5. 1990

    Hubble Space Telescope

    A telescope in orbit that changes astronomy.

  6. 1995

    First planet around another star

    51 Pegasi b is found.

  7. 1998

    Space station

    The first part of the ISS is launched. People have lived on it all the time since 2000.

2000 TO 2019

The age of robot explorers

  1. 2004

    Mars rovers and Cassini

    Spirit and Opportunity land on Mars. Cassini reaches Saturn.

  2. 2008

    Chandrayaan-1

    India's first Moon mission helps find signs of water on the Moon.

  3. 2012

    Curiosity

    A rover lands on Mars.

  4. 2014

    Mangalyaan

    India reaches Mars orbit on its first try.

  5. 2015

    New Horizons and LIGO

    A probe flies past Pluto. Gravitational waves are detected for the first time.

  6. 2019

    First black hole picture

    The Event Horizon Telescope shows the shadow of a black hole in M87.

2020 TO TODAY

The present

  1. 2021

    Webb and Perseverance

    Perseverance lands on Mars. The James Webb Space Telescope launches on 25 December.

  2. 2022

    DART and Artemis I

    A craft hits an asteroid to test planet defence. Artemis I goes around the Moon with no crew.

  3. 2023

    Chandrayaan-3 and Aditya-L1

    India lands near the Moon's south pole and launches its Sun mission.

  4. 2024

    Europa Clipper

    NASA sends a probe to Jupiter's icy moon Europa. China brings back far side Moon rocks.

  5. 2025

    Indian astronaut on the ISS

    Shubhanshu Shukla spends about 18 days on the space station.

  6. Now

    What is next

    Artemis, Gaganyaan, new telescopes and missions to Jupiter and Titan are in progress. Check NASA, ESA and ISRO for news.
